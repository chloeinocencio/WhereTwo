WhereTwo Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

WhereTwo helps you plan trips with the people you travel with. This policy explains what the WhereTwo iPhone app and website collect, why, and what you can do about it. WhereTwo does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a username you choose, and your password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Trips you create: destination, where you're staying, travel dates, interests, travel style and pace, the generated plan, any flights you add, and which people you've invited.
  • On your device only: which activities you've checked off, and map locations for the places in your plans. These stay on your iPhone and are not sent to us.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

How it's used

  • To sign you in and keep your trips in sync between your devices and the people you share them with.
  • To generate itineraries. Your trip's destination, stay, number of days, interests, style and pace are sent to Google Gemini to write the plan. Your email and username are not sent.
  • To send account emails, such as a welcome message or a notice that someone invited you to a trip.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts accounts and trip data.
  • Google (Gemini API) generates itineraries from the trip details above.
  • Resend delivers account emails.
  • Apple provides maps and place search in the iPhone app.

Sharing with other people

When you invite someone to a trip, they can see and edit that trip, and they see your username. People can only be invited by their username.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete account) or on the website. This permanently deletes your account and every trip you own, and removes you from trips others shared with you. Signing out also clears trips saved on that device.

Children

WhereTwo isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
