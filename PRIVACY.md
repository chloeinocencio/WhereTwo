# WhereTwo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

## What we collect

- **Account info:** your email address, a username, and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see your password).
- **Trip content:** the itineraries you create, including destinations, dates, and plans, and the usernames of people you invite to a trip.

## How it's used

- To run your account and save your trips so you can access them on any device.
- To share a trip with the people you invite. Invited users can see that trip's contents.
- **AI trip planning:** when you ask WhereTwo to generate a plan, your trip details (such as destination, dates, and preferences) are sent to Google's Gemini API to produce suggestions. We don't send your email or password. Google processes this data under its own terms.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
- We don't use third-party analytics or tracking.

## Where it's stored

Account and trip data are stored with Supabase, our hosting and database provider.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account anytime in the app's account settings. This permanently removes your account and the trips you own.
